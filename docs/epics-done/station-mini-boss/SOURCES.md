# Level 1 space-station mini-boss — sources

Merged and de-duplicated from the six `2-research.md` files in the epic's plan directories. The
*Sub-item* column says which cycle consulted the source; where two cycles read the same page for
different reasons, both are listed.

Three sources did the heavy lifting across the whole epic and are worth reading before touching
this boss again: **Giest118's bullet-hell boss guide** (pacing and the no-randomness rule),
**Boghog's shmup 101** (layering, chunking, spawn lanes) and **Sparen's Danmakufu tutorials**
(telegraph and ring geometry). Everything else is corroboration or a single number.

| [Giest118's Guide to Making Good Bullet Hell Bosses](https://shmups.system11.org/viewtopic.php?t=44816) — 403 to `WebFetch`, recovered with `scripts/fetch-page.sh` | 3, 4a, 4b, 5 | "A stage 1 boss shouldn't exceed 40 to 50 seconds"; hold one attack "no more than five to ten seconds"; **"Don't decide attack orders based on randomness"**, with the worked failure case; keep attack density constant and vary trajectory by less than the bullet spacing; total-run budget of ~25 min. | The whole fight's sizing (30–60 s). `laser_volley_interval = 6.5`, `core_ring_interval = 2.0`, `turret_fire_interval = 1.8`, `reinforcement_interval = 10.0` all sit in the 5–10 s event band. The no-randomness rule is why **every** attack in this boss is a fixed sequence — `test_volley_angles_are_deterministic`, `test_volleys_are_deterministic`, `test_squads_cycle_left_right_bottom_top_and_then_repeat`, `test_blast_offsets_are_deterministic`. Also why the death sequence is 1.8 s, not 4. |
| [Boghog's bullet hell shmup 101](https://shmups.wiki/library/Boghog%27s_bullet_hell_shmup_101) | 1, 3, 4a, 4b, 5 | Optimal/average/slow-kill design; simultaneous patterns must be visually distinguishable; **aimed patterns "apply pressure while allowing conscious manipulation", static ones "are good for creating obstacles"**; "chunking patterns is vital for visibility… single stray bullets are hard to read and can often feel unfair"; popcorn is a *layer* over a core and should have minimal HP; the Toaplan alternate-sides rule, 5–7 lanes, and **"the edges of the screen don't have lanes to prevent awkward traps"**; bullets are cancelled on death "to clear up the screen". | Turrets fire **aimed** fans (pressure) while the core ring is **fixed-angle** (obstacle) — the layering in `station_gunnery.gd`. `turret_burst_count = 3` is the chunking rule. Reinforcement squads are popcorn only (`interceptor` / `kamikaze_drone` / `fighter`, never `gunship`), alternate sides L→R→B→T, and spawn at design y = ±20/80 rather than hugging a border. `BulletPool.cancel_active()` on death comes from the bullet-cancel courtesy. |

This is the single most load-bearing number in the epic: the plan's original `0.21` gives `3 × 0.21 ≈ 0.63` against a `0.6283` spacing, collapsing the field onto three lanes. `test_the_ring_step_leaves_no_permanent_safe_lane` pins it. |
| [Retro Game Deconstruction Zone — Reaction Time and Game Design](https://www.retrogamedeconstructionzone.com/2020/05/reaction-time-and-game-design.html) | 3 | Simple visual cue → **200–300 ms** response; ~15 frames at 60 fps as a working baseline; asking for more than ~20 frames of total response is an unreasonable demand. | The *floor* the telegraph is measured against, not the target. The shipped 1.9–2.0 s tell is ~6× it — deliberately, because during a boss phase the player is also tracking bullets and their own ship. |
| [ULTRAKILL Wiki — Mindflayer](https://ultrakill.fandom.com/wiki/Mindflayer) — HTTP 402 to `WebFetch`, recovered with `scripts/fetch-page.sh` | 3 | A shipped sweeping-beam attack documented frame-exactly: **constant angular velocity**, 1.0 s total, leads the player by 0.25 s, and a thin indicator line that marks only where the beam **starts** — the wiki says outright it is not accurate to the beam's path. A 2021 patch note reads "Mindflayer beam warning flash changed to blue". | `laser_rotation_speed = 0.5` rad/s is a **constant** rate with no easing. The finding that a sweep's telegraph only has to say *which way to run* is why the station reuses `LaserRay`'s existing warning line instead of growing a predictive path preview. The patched warning colour is why the warning had to be visually distinct at all. |
| [shmups.wiki — Help:Dodging strategy](https://shmups.wiki/library/Help:Dodging_strategy) | 3 | Rotating / oscillating / curving bullets are read as "large swathes… their entire trajectory treated as one large threat zone that should be avoided"; even chaotic patterns must leave gaps to weave through. | `laser_beam_count = 2`, opposed. Two beams sweep the plane while always leaving two large clear quadrants; four at 90° leave the player nowhere to stand that is not about to be swept. |

Star Fox's Great Commander with **six** small turret hitboxes is cited as the pain point, eventually forcing players to risk collision damage. | The whole armour rule, the decision to keep the core's `HurtBox` **live** while rejecting damage, **four** generously-sized turrets rather than six small ones, and the deliberate **rejection** of escalation (it would cancel out the 4→3→2→1 quietening that is the player's reward). |

## Judgement calls with no citable source

Labelled plainly here so nobody later mistakes them for research-backed figures. Each is also
labelled in the plan directory it came from.

| Decision | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Beam sweep rate | `laser_rotation_speed = 0.5` rad/s (~29°/s) | **No source gives degrees/second.** Derived from this game's own numbers: at the ~400 px the player sits from the hull, `ω·r` puts the beam edge at 200 px/s — half the player's 400 px/s top speed (`move_state.gd:21`), so outrunning it sideways is a decision rather than a reflex. Research contributed only "constant angular velocity". |
| Death-sequence length and shape | 1.8 s, 7 blasts ~0.22 s apart, then one 2× central blast | **No source gives a number for a mini-boss death.** Seven reads as a chain, three reads as a hiccup; 1.8 s is short enough not to be resented on a fifth retry. Comfortably inside the 180 s section timeout so it can never race the safety net. |
| Bullet density | `turret_fire_interval 1.8` × `turret_burst_count 3` = 6.7 bullets/s at full strength | Sparen A4 explicitly **refuses** a bullet-count threshold ("Fewer Bullets != Lower Difficulty"). Derived from measured project constants — player 400 px/s, arena bounds in `enemy_bullet.gd`, shipped enemy bullet speeds. |
| Reinforcement cadence and cap | first at 8 s, then every 10 s, max 4 alive | The 8 s opening (boss-only introduction) and the cap are project judgement; only the 5–10 s band itself is sourced (Giest118). |
| Wreckage handling on a destroyed turret | swap to a destroyed sprite | **No source found either way** in the reachable pages. Treated as an open aesthetic choice and taken as the cheap option; sub-item 1's research says so rather than claiming backing. |
| Phase 2 has no aimed component | — | A deliberate acceptance, recorded as a risk in `station-bullet-hell/3-plan.md`: the core ring is fixed-angle and the beams are static-in-kind, so nothing in phase 2 aims. Justified only because the hull rotates, the ring precesses and the beams sweep, so no position is safe *over time*. Unverified in play. |
